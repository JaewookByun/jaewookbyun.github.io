Theme 01: Clocks & Time

From Second Hands to Sun & Moon,
Hands-On Discovery of Time.

Beyond dry memorization. Children explore how gear-linked second, minute, and hour hands interact, and watch the sky shift from morning sun to moonlit night.

  • Interlocking Gear Mechanics: 60 seconds rotate 1 minute, and 60 minutes advance 1 hour—making time's cause and effect crystal clear.
  • Sun & Moon 24-Hour Cycle: Rotating clock hands triggers dynamic sky shifts, effortlessly teaching AM/PM and 24-hour daily rhythms.
  • Mastering Tricky "Minutes Before": Progressive mastery from exact hours and 5-minute ticks to challenging "minutes to" concepts.
  • 1-Minute Speed Dash & Hall of Fame: Fast-paced 60-second quiz challenges with combo multipliers and family-friendly leaderboards.
